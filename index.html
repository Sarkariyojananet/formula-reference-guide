1. Systematic Investment Plan (SIP) Future Value Open Interactive SIP Calculator →

FV = P × [((1 + r)^n - 1) / r] × (1 + r)
  • P: Monthly investment amount
  • r: Periodic monthly rate of return (Annual Rate / 12 / 100)
  • n: Total number of monthly installments (Years × 12)

Example: Investing 5,000/month for 10 years (n = 120) at 12% p.a. (r = 0.01) yields ~11,61,695.

2. Loan Equated Monthly Installment (EMI) Open Interactive EMI Calculator →

EMI = [P × R × (1 + R)^N] / [(1 + R)^N - 1]
  • P: Principal loan amount
  • R: Monthly interest rate (Annual Rate / 12 / 100)
  • N: Loan tenure in months

3. Concrete Volume & Dry Mix Material Estimation Open Concrete Calculator →

Dry Volume = Wet Volume × 1.54

To account for voids in sand and aggregate, wet concrete volume is multiplied by 1.54 before applying nominal mix ratios:

Grade Mix Ratio (Cement : Sand : Aggregate) Typical Application
M151 : 2 : 4Plain Cement Concrete (PCC)
M201 : 1.5 : 3Residential Slabs, Beams & Footings
M251 : 1 : 2Heavy Structural Columns & Beams

4. Reinforcement Steel Unit Weight (IS 1786) Open RCC Beam Steel Calculator →

Unit Weight (kg/m) = D² / 162.2
  • D: Diameter of steel rebar in millimeters (mm)
  • Example: For a 12 mm bar: (12 × 12) / 162.2 = 0.888 kg/m

5. Percentage & Percentage Change Open Percentage Calculator →

Percentage (%) = (Part / Whole) × 100
Percentage Change = ((New Value - Old Value) / Old Value) × 100

6. Body Mass Index (BMI) Open BMI Calculator →

BMI = Weight (kg) / [Height (m)]²
  • Underweight: < 18.5
  • Normal: 18.5 – 24.9
  • Overweight: 25.0 – 29.9
